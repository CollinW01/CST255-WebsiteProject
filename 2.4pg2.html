Application Systems Analyst

Currently, I work as an application systems analyst for Avita Health System with my main program being Dimensional Insight (DI).
DI receives data exports daily and manipulates those data fields to build reports and databases. These are used by many of the administrative positions to see information such as patient volumes, revenue and user specific metrics.

Regional Manager for Subway

My last job was managing four Subway locations.
In this position, I ensured all managers were fulfilling their duties, stepped in when they were unable to, and took care of maintenance needs.
The manager position orders truck, creates schedules, counts inventory, as well as other general daily practices.

Subway was my first job. I started out as a Sandwich Artist and worked my way up over the years. Fast food, specifically made-to-order restaurants, is interesting because of the amount of interaction with the public. I got to know many of the regular customers over the years and learned a lot about myself. There are aspects of this kind of job that are not fun, but for the most part I enjoyed my time at Subway.
