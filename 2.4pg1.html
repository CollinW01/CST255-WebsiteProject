These hobbies give me a good balance between spending time outside, working with technology, and continuing to practice music. Each hobby also gives me something different to learn and improve over time.

Golfing

I am still a little new to golfing.
This is my third year playing and there is still plenty to learn.
Currently my handicap is 18 but I have felt much better about all my clubs this year. Golf has been a very fun sport to get into, every slight deviation can make a huge difference when your shot is landing 200 yards away, even heat is enough to make a noticeable difference in ball flight.

Technology

I have a Raspberry Pi ad blocker for my network and have setup a home server for media and remote gaming/3D printing. I have also built a few computers and have a smart home setup.

Music

Music has been a big part of my life. In high school I played trombone for many different kinds of events.
I was in the Northwest Regional Orchestra and District 2 honor band and I currently play for the North Coast Concert Band (See hyperlink).
I was a recipient of the John Phillip Sousa Award for my school and I also play piano fairly regularly in my free time.
